INDUSTRIAL VIBRATION TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

Vibration is an important indicator of mechanical machine condition.

Potential causes of elevated vibration include:

1. Bearing wear
2. Shaft misalignment
3. Rotor imbalance
4. Loose mounting
5. Coupling problems
6. Poor lubrication
7. Gear wear

Recommended inspection sequence:

1. Check recent vibration history.
2. Inspect bearings.
3. Check shaft and coupling alignment.
4. Inspect mounting bolts.
5. Verify lubrication.
6. Inspect rotating components.
7. Compare vibration measurements with historical values.

If vibration remains elevated after basic maintenance, perform a detailed condition assessment.

Vibration should be evaluated together with temperature, operating hours, maintenance history, and previous failure information.
