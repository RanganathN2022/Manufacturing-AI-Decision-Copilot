INDUSTRIAL MACHINE TEMPERATURE GUIDE

Elevated temperature can be an indicator of abnormal machine operation.

Potential causes include:

1. Bearing friction
2. Insufficient lubrication
3. Cooling system problems
4. Motor overload
5. Excessive mechanical friction
6. Electrical problems

Recommended inspection sequence:

1. Verify temperature measurement.
2. Compare current temperature with historical values.
3. Inspect cooling system.
4. Check lubrication.
5. Inspect bearings.
6. Check motor operating condition.
7. Review recent maintenance activities.

Temperature should not be interpreted in isolation.

Combine temperature information with vibration, operating hours, machine age, maintenance history, and electrical measurements when evaluating machine condition.
